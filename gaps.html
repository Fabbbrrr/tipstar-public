This is a working pipeline for rating AUS-style greyhound races against a value model, with real Sportsbet and Ladbrokes data adapters wired in, a Task Scheduler installer for hands-off weekly tips + nightly settle, cross-book price overlay, and a concept-drift gate.

The following were left out on purpose or are still open:

  1. No notification channel is configured. report.send() prints the

formatted report unless TIPSTAR_WEBHOOK (or --webhook) is set — there is no default URL, Discord server, or audience wired up anywhere. The weekly job will honour TIPSTAR_WEBHOOK if you set it in the environment before running install.bat.

  1. No legal, licensing, or compliance review. The data endpoints used

here are public (no auth), but the bookmakers' terms of service, any data-licensing requirements, and responsible-gambling / advertising rules that might apply to distributing tips in your jurisdiction have NOT been assessed. Confirm all of that independently before pointing this at anything live or sharing its output with anyone else.

  1. No fitted or validated model out of the box. model.EXAMPLE_COEF is

an inert placeholder (see docs/MODEL.md) so --mock --dry runs clean. A real model requires at least ~20 settled races in the training window; install.bat runs a one-time 13-day backfill + train in a detached window the first time you use it, so the model is ready by the time the first nightly job fires. Until then, tips will score on the placeholder.

  1. Drift gate has a warm-up period. tipstar.drift compares the model's

log-loss on the most recent 3 days against a 7-day baseline; with fewer than ~10 usable cards in the recent window it reports insufficient data rather than a ratio. Expect the first two or three weeks of nightly runs to log insufficient data before it starts producing a real ratio and acting on a threshold breach.

  1. Cross-book overlay only fires on qualifying tips. The weekly job

loads the secondary book only to fetch a card for the ~1–6 tips that cleared the promotion gate, matching on advertised start time + runner name overlap (verified live 2026-09-03: Sportsbet + Ladbrokes name and box the same dog identically; the better of the two win prices is what gets recorded). Races that both books price but the model does not tip on are not cross-referenced — that is fine, because those are precisely the tips we are not acting on.

Model outputs are relative-value estimates from an unvalidated model — not outcome predictions and not financial or betting advice.
